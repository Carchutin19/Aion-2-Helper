✨ Aion 2 Helper v1.29.0 — Menu Fades & Hotbar Improvements

🪄 Your overlays now follow the game’s UI
Gameplay widgets fade out when supported full-screen menus or NPC dialogues hide Aion’s HUD, then fade back in when you return. Notifications remain available, and unlocked widgets stay visible for editing.

⚔️ Hotbar improvements
Improved supported Assassin skill stacks and macros, including moved skills and fourth-row handling. Infiltrate now supports its flight activation and loses that activation when you fold your wings.

🖼️ In-game HUD presets
The Helper can reacquire the supported potion-bar key labels after you switch HUD layouts, instead of assuming the bar has stayed in the same place.

🛠️ HUD copier improvements
“Create HUD without hotbar” now supports non-empty exports with omitted skill-bar entries. Your original export and the rest of your exported layout are preserved.

💎 Odyle Energy — Coming soon
A new sidebar entry is visible, but it stays unavailable until automatic tracking is ready.
